# “Nanobanana” image prompts (optional)

Use these if you want quick visuals for slides. Copy/paste into your image tool.

## 1) Simple cloud architecture diagram (clean, flat)
Prompt:
> Create a clean, flat vector-style diagram on a white background. Show a "Step Functions" box in the centre connected to three boxes in sequence: "Preprocess Lambda", "Embed/Model Lambda (bottleneck)", "Postprocess Lambda". Add a small "CloudWatch Dashboard" icon on the side connected with dotted lines to all three boxes. Add a label above: "Scale or Fail — Ticket Triage Pipeline". Use minimal text, tidy spacing, and simple AWS-like icons without logos.

## 2) The spine as a poster
Prompt:
> A minimalist poster design with three bold lines of text, centred: "Scaling is the job." "Orchestration is the mechanism." "Root Cause Analysis is the safety net." Add a subtle background pattern suggesting network traffic. Clean, professional, modern typography.

## 3) Fishbone diagram (scalability)
Prompt:
> A simple fishbone diagram on a white background. The head says "Pipeline slow/failing under load". The four main bones are labeled: "Limits", "Resources", "Dependencies", "Data/Input". Under each bone, include two short example sub-causes (e.g., under Limits: "concurrency cap", "quota"; under Resources: "memory too low", "CPU too low"; under Dependencies: "slow API", "downstream throttling"; under Data/Input: "payload too large", "schema mismatch"). Keep it readable and uncluttered.
